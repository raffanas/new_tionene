## A. Confirmed architectural decisions

### DEC-001 — Linked records, independent editing, selected synchronization — CONFIRMED

- **Approved:** Master catalogs → contract → arrival / services → supplier payables and client wallet are linked, but once records are created, each operational/financial/wallet record can be individually edited without silent mutation of the original contract or reference data.
- **Approved:** After an operational record is amended, offer **optional/selective downstream updates** to relevant other records. Staff may choose to keep them different. A provider may change date by WhatsApp; an admin can manually edit its confirmation directly without forcing use of booking link, preserving code/pickup information that has not changed.
- **Rationale:** Frequent last-minute provider, customer and price changes; the business must never be blocked by rigid synchronization.
- **Do now:** Preserve independent values and support controlled updates of linked records.
- **v0.1.40 current resolution:** Change prompt, arbitrary subset selection, sticky overrides, nonblocking mismatch and audit/attention semantics are specified by `11_*`; exact dependency implementation and atypical conflicts remain developer engineering/UAT topics.

### DEC-002 — Draft, finalization, revision and one arrival — CONFIRMED

- **Approved:** Seller can save and resume a **draft**; a draft has **no** active arrival, bookings, booking links, supplier ledger rows or wallet.
- **Approved:** The seller deliberately clicks **Finalize Sale** when they determine the customer has agreed; that action creates the active contract version/PDF, one arrival, sold tour/service reservations and initial planned supplier costs. Customer signing is tracked separately and is not a prerequisite to opening the operational workflow. Previewing/exporting a PDF cannot trigger finalization, and repeated finalization/PDF exports must not produce duplicates. See `DEC-009`.
- **Approved:** Seller can revise an issued contract; preserve earlier PDFs/signed versions. New version stays on the **same** sale/arrival. Reconcile simple untouched downstream records automatically. **Do not wipe and recreate** confirmed bookings, paid rows, manually overridden records or history; exceptions require operations review.
- **Rationale:** Sellers sometimes amend agreements after sale, generally **before** booking/settlement but not invariably.
- **Do now:** Versioned contract lifecycle, non-destructive delta processing, restricted seller permissions.
- **Still open:** signature workflow, financial adjustments after revised total, draft/finalization validation, exact protected-conflict UX (`OQ-01`–`OQ-05`, `OQ-27`).

### DEC-003 — Flexible provider reservation and review — CONFIRMED

- **Approved:** Every operational service/tour offers its own copyable provider reservation link, but **supplier use of the link is optional**. Admin may directly enter/update booking details and mark supplier-confirmed from WhatsApp, phone or other communication.
- **Approved:** **No universal requirement** for confirmation code, pickup place or pickup time. A yacht provider can respond “yes” without any of them. Staff review is **optional but clearly visible** and distinct from provider confirmation.
- **Approved:** A sold item can require more than one operational provider service. For current yacht scenario, staff **manually adds** the transfer within the arrival; separate provider reservation/payable, no extra sold line automatically.
- **Rationale:** Different suppliers have different booking and pickup processes; included ancillary work may be performed by a second provider.
- **Do now:** Optional booking-link workflow, direct manual confirmations, visible review state, staff-created ancillary bookings.
- **Still open:** booking-status transition rules beyond the five approved labels in `DEC-013` and the supplier-link confirmation trigger in `DEC-012`, link privacy, provider rejection/no-reply and extra-service attribution (`OQ-11`–`OQ-14`, `OQ-29`, `OQ-36`).

### DEC-004 — Planned provider cost at finalization; configurable reports — CONFIRMED

- **Approved:** At sale finalization, create supplier financial rows as **planned/estimated** even before actual reservation; as provider booking progresses, update the **same** financial relationship, not a duplicate.
- **Approved:** Users can choose **planned, confirmed and paid** cost measures in reports individually or together. Distinguish them and never double count a supplier charge. Provider ledger amounts can be manually changed independently from upstream records.
- **Rationale:** Owner needs advance cost visibility and accurate financial commitments; many provider tariffs have one-off exceptions.
- **Do now:** Planned entries and distinct measures/statuses; payable editing; configurable measures and provider/time filters.
- **Still open:** due/obligation timing, partial/batch settlement, costs vs cashflow, currencies, refunds, revenue allocation (`OQ-19`–`OQ-24`).

### DEC-005 — Editable internal digital wallet + PDF — CONFIRMED

- **Approved:** Each arrival has independently editable client-facing wallet data in internal app. Staff can generate/export a PDF and manually share via WhatsApp group, email or other channel; later revised PDFs can be generated.
- **Approved:** Possible customer-facing website login/portal **is not decided**. It might be in **this same phase or later**. It must not be prematurely classified as postponed to a particular phase.
- **Do now:** Design/store wallet separately from PDF renderer so a future guest portal can reuse it. Do **not** build or implicitly rule out portal based on this version.
- **Still open:** portal, final wallet formatting/content, language, access and distribution status (`OQ-25`, `OQ-30`).

### DEC-006 — Incomplete wallet output is allowed — CONFIRMED

- **Approved:** Staff may generate/export/share a PDF even with pending or unconfirmed tours. Show those items visibly as **pending/unconfirmed**, warn about incomplete itinerary/confirmations, **do not block**.
- **Rationale:** Real operational need to communicate partial itineraries.
- **Do now:** Clear per-service confirmation state and warning on PDF generation; no fake confirmed itinerary.
- **Still open:** exact incomplete-content warning and presentation (`OQ-30`, `OQ-38`).

### DEC-007 — Automatically derived operational action queue — CONFIRMED

- **Approved:** System identifies and prioritizes outstanding reservations and required work **automatically**. A staff member need not create normal booking-follow-up tasks manually. Urgency considers **arrival date and individual service date**, including bookings made after arrival or one day before tour.
- **Further confirmed:** Staff need **BOTH** a clear per-arrival **Ready / Not Ready** indicator on the arrivals overview/list **AND** the derived **Action Required** list that explains what needs doing. Selecting a Not Ready arrival exposes its outstanding operational issues. Do not offer these as mutually exclusive alternatives or force staff to maintain two independent task/status systems (`OPS-005`, `OPS-006`, `SC-068`–`SC-069`).
- **Do now:** Action Required queue derived from outstanding/near-term booking data; arrival-level readiness indicator computed from recorded operational issues; today/tomorrow services and arrivals views.
- **Further confirmed:** **Operational readiness and customer payment status are separate.** Outstanding or overdue client money must **not** by itself force an otherwise operationally complete arrival to Not Ready; show the amount due/overdue as its own clearly visible financial warning (`OPS-007`, `REC-007`, `SC-070`).
- **Further confirmed:** **Wallet preparation is an operational readiness condition; sending is not.** An arrival may be **Ready** as soon as its wallet is current and ready to send and the *other* operational criteria (still to be defined) are satisfied. Do **not** require sending, PDF delivery verification or Mark as Sent for the Ready badge; retain distinct lightweight send/reminder indicator (`OPS-008`, `WAL-008`, `SC-071`).
- **Further confirmed:** A **known included ancillary booking requirement** (e.g. transport to a yacht) must be flagged in Action Required **before staff create its separate reservation**. Creating/arranging the linked service clears that specific missing-booking flag; any subsequent supplier-confirmation action remains distinct. A known missing required service prevents Ready. Staff still create the actual subordinate reservation manually (`CAT-010`, `OPS-009`, `OQ-14`, `OQ-26`).
- **Still open:** remaining **operational** readiness threshold(s) and exception handling, reminder lead times, overdue thresholds, channels/notification methods, assignment/ownership (`OQ-26`, `OQ-36`). Calendar presentation is undecided; do not require it before implementing list/readiness/queue.

### DEC-008 — Airport transfers, agency zone and supplier tariff-zone overrides — CONFIRMED

- **Approved:** Airport transfers are bookable services. Seller chooses hotel; software auto-selects agency hotel's geographic zone/default applicable cost where tariff data exists, reducing seller work.
- **Approved:** Agency has **one curated canonical zone** per hotel. The **same** hotel may be charged as a different tariff zone by different providers. A complete provider × hotel lookup is **not** needed for Phase 1.
- **Approved:** In a particular operational booking, staff may either (a) override provider **billing/tariff zone**, using that provider's zone price if known, or (b) keep zone as-is and override just the cost. Neither changes source hotel zone, reference tariffs, contract or other bookings. Offer optional sync to provider ledger.
- **Rationale:** Compiling a full provider-specific mapping of 1,000+ hotels across all suppliers is not practical now.
- **Do now:** One agency hotel zone/default, localized tariff-zone/cost override, manual price fallback; future provider-specific lookup extensibility.
- **Still open:** unusual flight/leg variants, combined client selling-price attribution, hotel switches, and tariffs for providers/routes not yet covered by Miguel/Playa Mía rules (`OQ-07`, `OQ-09`, `OQ-10`).

### DEC-009 — Explicit seller-controlled sale finalization — CONFIRMED

- **Approved:** The seller chooses when a sale is agreed and clicks a distinct **Finalize Sale** action. Only this transition (not creating, previewing or downloading a PDF, and not customer signature/return of signed contract) activates the sale and creates its single arrival, sold-service booking records, provider links, initial planned supplier costs and operational follow-up.
- **Approved:** Finalized means the agreed sale has entered the operational workflow; it **does not mean permanently immutable**. A client can later change their itinerary. Seller may issue V2/V3 contract versions, preserve historic contract/PDF/signed copies and non-destructively reconcile safe downstream changes as approved in `DEC-002`; protected provider-confirmed, paid or manually edited records require operations review.
- **Rationale:** Sales can be agreed before signed contracts are returned, and changes after signing or issuing a contract are normal. Staff must choose the business commitment explicitly; a PDF preview is not a commitment trigger.
- **Do now:** Explicit authorized seller Finalize Sale action, distinct draft/finalized states and idempotent one-time operational initialization; retain contract revisions after that point. Simple seller signed-file upload is approved separately in `DEC-010`, with electronic signing and legal validation still open under `OQ-03`.
- **Still open:** Beyond-basic signed-file upload (`DEC-010`, `OQ-03`), draft validation (`OQ-04`), permissions beyond approved seller boundary (`OQ-28`), handling of financial changes on revised contracts (`OQ-02`, `OQ-22`). Do not invent a mandatory signature or deposit gate.

### DEC-010 — Manual signed-contract upload, separate from sale finalization — CONFIRMED

- **Approved:** Sellers can **upload a returned signed contract file** through a simple upload action on the relevant contract record. Signed files are tracked against the corresponding contract **version** and earlier signed files/versions remain preserved.
- **Approved:** Collecting or uploading a signed contract is **independent of Finalize Sale**; it does not start, stop, repeat or reverse operational initialization. A finalized sale can already have reservations and payables before the signed document is received. Contract revisions remain possible under `DEC-002`.
- **Rationale:** The existing business receives signed paperwork separately, often after the seller regards the sale as agreed. Staff needs practical file storage, not mandatory new signing technology.
- **Do now:** Provide role-authorized signed-file upload and retrieval per contract version, without forcing e-signatures or automatic legal status claims. Keep the upload workflow simple.
- **Still open (`OQ-03`):** Electronic signature features, document authenticity/validity verification, accepted file types/limits, whose signature is required, and whether/how a later revised version needs a new signature. **Do not assume a signed upload legally validates the agreement.**

### DEC-011 — Only Seller and Admin roles; sellers share contract access — CONFIRMED

- **Approved:** For Phase 1, keep exactly two roles: **Seller** and **Admin**. Do not introduce additional role types now.
- **Approved:** Sellers may **view, create, finalize and revise contracts created by other sellers**, not only their own. Previously approved draft management and version-specific signed-document upload remain available.
- **Approved boundary:** This does **not** expand seller access to unrestricted company financial reports, supplier ledgers/costs, global operational booking administration or other sensitive back-office modules. Admin is the broadly authorized managerial/operational role.
- **Rationale:** Shared sales work needs simple team access without the administrative burden of fine-grained role design at this stage.
- **Do now:** Support cross-seller contract lookup and revision for Seller; keep all finalizations/revisions linked to the correct existing sale and arrival; enforce basic Seller/Admin separation for protected finance and administration.
- **Still open:** The detailed Seller/Admin screen/field/action access matrix, permissions for client payments, arrivals/wallets and personally identifying data (`OQ-28`, `OQ-35`). Do not ask the owner to define additional roles now or silently grant any unapproved privileges.

### DEC-012 — Provider link confirms booking; neither confirmation nor staff review gates downstream — CONFIRMED

- **Approved:** A provider's submission of its individual reservation link automatically marks **that booking** as **Provider Confirmed**. It does **not** mark **Staff Reviewed**; review remains unchecked until a staff member independently performs that optional review.
- **Approved:** Staff may also directly record provider confirmation from WhatsApp, telephone or other communication, whether or not the provider link is used. Confirmation code, pickup time and pickup place are **not universally required**; their absence must not prevent marking a legitimate booking confirmed.
- **Approved:** **Neither Provider Confirmed nor Staff Reviewed is a prerequisite** for normal downstream processing. Finalize Sale still creates the arrival, planned provider costs and operational records before any supplier reply. Payables, reporting, the editable guest wallet, PDF output and other downstream records can exist while bookings remain unconfirmed. Wallet output must honestly identify pending bookings, warn about incomplete confirmation and remain exportable.
- **Rationale:** Some suppliers never use the link, or confirm informally and provide no code/pickup details. Their reservations and accounting must remain manageable without artificial status gates.
- **Do now:** Implement these independent states and transitions, nonblocking flow, and correct pending/confirmed presentation. Provider form submissions affect **only the intended booking** and cannot set another service's staff-review status.
- **Still open:** The labels are settled in `DEC-013` and manual provider-outreach recording in `DEC-014`; refusal/cancellation/reopening transitions, resubmission behavior and any later service-completion concept remain open (`OQ-13`, `OQ-29`, `OQ-36`). Do not invent a full state machine.

### DEC-013 — Five provider-booking statuses — CONFIRMED

- **Approved:** The individual operational/provider booking uses these **five business statuses**: **Not Requested**, **Awaiting Confirmation**, **Confirmed**, **Unable to Confirm**, **Cancelled**. `Confirmed` is the provider-confirmed state used in Decisions 3 and 12. **Staff Reviewed** is a separate optional indicator, not a sixth booking status.
- **Approved carry-forward:** Provider submission of its unique reservation link moves the **target booking** to Confirmed, without marking it Staff Reviewed. Authorized staff can also record a real external confirmation manually. None of these statuses gate creation of the planned supplier payable, reporting, or a pending client wallet.
- **Rationale:** Staff can distinguish work that has never been requested, work pending a supplier response, confirmed bookings, unavailable bookings and cancellations without a complex status vocabulary.
- **Do now:** Use these five labels consistently in individual bookings, arrival summaries and action-needed views. Do not automatically infer a provider was contacted merely because a booking link was generated or copied, and do not treat a booking record as already Confirmed on sale finalization.
- **Still open (`OQ-13`, `OQ-36`):** Other status transitions/permissions; when to use Unable to Confirm; whether/how to reopen or replace cancelled bookings; provider cancellation fees; service completion/no-show representation and alerts. `DEC-014` defines the manual **Mark as Requested** action. A separate *Completed* status was **not approved**. No invented automatic transitions, refunds or cancellation liability changes.

### DEC-014 — Manual Mark as Requested after provider outreach — CONFIRMED

- **Approved:** Each operational/provider reservation offers a lightweight **Mark as Requested** action. After staff have actually contacted the supplier (e.g., by sending its copied reservation link through WhatsApp or requesting the booking directly in a message), they can use this action to change **Not Requested → Awaiting Confirmation** for that specific reservation.
- **Approved:** Merely generating, viewing or copying a provider link **does not** mean it was delivered and must **not** change the booking status. The system cannot assume an external WhatsApp message was sent unless a future verified integration supports that claim; there is no such integration requirement in this decision.
- **Approved carry-forward:** The provider's submitted link or staff-recorded genuine supplier acceptance sets **Confirmed** independently; **Staff Reviewed** remains separate and optional. Recording outreach is **not** a prerequisite for wallet/PDF, supplier planned costs, financial reports, or other downstream data (`DEC-012`).
- **Rationale:** Lightweight user action gives the operational dashboard a reliable distinction between bookings not yet requested and supplier responses still awaited, without forcing provider link usage.
- **Do now:** Provide the explicit action on relevant operational booking views, update only that booking's status to **Awaiting Confirmation**, and let the automated action queue distinguish it from **Not Requested**. No requirement to log a copied-link event as dispatch.
- **Still open (`OQ-13`, `OQ-36`):** Whether staff may undo/re-send a request, capturing a contact timestamp/channel, reminders and timeouts, transitions after refusal/cancellation, and provider communication integrations. Do not invent these rules.

### DEC-015 — Manual unavailable-booking resolution; independently editable/removable bookings and related expenses — CONFIRMED

- **Approved:** When a supplier tells staff they cannot accommodate a booking, staff can mark that booking **Unable to Confirm**. It remains visible under the arrival and is surfaced prominently in **Action Required** until resolved. The app does not pretend to detect unintegrated WhatsApp/phone conversations automatically. No alternative reservation is fabricated.
- **Approved:** Staff can **edit, remove from the active arrival, or replace** an individual operational booking manually, including changing date/tour/provider. The business needs a lightweight **Delete Reservation** control, not a forced preservation-heavy screen. Removing and manually creating another booking is an acceptable replacement path; no special automated replacement wizard has been approved.
- **Approved:** Any meaningful edit/removal offers a simple **selective downstream-impact choice**, including relevant provider payable/expense and internal digital wallet data. Unselected linked records can deliberately remain different. Edits or removal must **not automatically amend the original contract or master catalogs**, or silently overwrite independent financial adjustments.
- **Approved:** Deleting an operational reservation does **not** require deleting its associated provider finance record. For an **unpaid planned** amount, staff may remove the now-unneeded planned charge or retain it as an independently managed provider expense (e.g. a cancellation fee). **Actual recorded supplier payments must not be erased** by booking removal; retain paid expenses/history. Reports derive from current retained financial records rather than needing a separate manual 'update reports' toggle.
- **Approved safeguard:** Internal deletion is **not** confirmation that an external supplier booking was cancelled. If a confirmed provider booking is being removed, surface a concise warning. Keep minimal behind-the-scenes recoverability/linkage to avoid broken financial references without cluttering day-to-day screens with audit history.
- **Rationale:** Staff frequently need to change real-world arrangements and costs; a rejected booking must be actionable, but historic payments and genuine fees remain attributable even if the operational itinerary changes.
- **Do now:** Support staff-set **Unable to Confirm** and derived Action Required visibility, booking-local edit and removal, selective linked financial/wallet impact, and retained independent expenses. For a deleted booking with a retained payable, keep it valid as an expense associated with its provider/arrival where available, not a dangling 'active reservation'.
- **Still open (`OQ-13`, `OQ-15`, `OQ-16`, `OQ-19`, `OQ-20`, `OQ-36`):** Exact edit/delete confirmation layout and which downstream checkboxes are preselected; deleting entries that contain a mix of paid and unpaid amounts; external provider cancellation/fee/refund rules; repeat booking/replace workflow; 'resolved' queue behavior for bookings removed vs left as Unable to Confirm; allowable status transitions beyond those specifically approved. Do not introduce new booking-status labels or invent supplier cancellation/refund actions.

### DEC-016 — One current wallet PDF, simple manual replacement and sent indicator — CONFIRMED

- **Approved:** Staff edit the internal per-arrival wallet, generate/download the **latest** PDF, share it manually with clients (WhatsApp/email/other) and use a simple **Mark as Sent** action. When wallet content is changed after the last marked-sent PDF, surface a small **Wallet Update Needed** reminder.
- **Approved:** The normal admin view shows **only the current wallet**. Do **not** show clients' PDF wallet V1, V2, V3 or a visible version list, detailed historical activity trail or per-edit attribution in everyday screens.
- **Rationale:** Business changes are frequent; staff need to give the client the latest itinerary without managing versions or extra administration. A previously sent PDF cannot be remotely replaced or silently updated.
- **Do now:** Keep wallet as editable underlying data and render one current PDF; offer a clear update-needed/sent state based on wallet changes, with manual acknowledgment after external sending. Minimal hidden technical recovery/version metadata is permitted but must not clutter UI; no claim that an actual WhatsApp/email send was verified.
- **Still open:** final content/format/language of client PDF, exact rules for accidental acknowledgment or retracting a send, portal and automatic delivery extensions (`OQ-25`, `OQ-30`).

### DEC-017 — Manual supplier payments, combined allocation and partial settlement — CONFIRMED

- **Approved:** Tío Nenê will manually record **actual outgoing supplier payments** made by **cash or bank transfer** (no connected bank feed). Staff can enter the supplier/payee, actual payment date, payment amount and **which outstanding reservation/payable entries this payment covers**. One payment can settle multiple bookings; a booking can be paid partly or through several payments.
- **Approved:** The supplier's *planned/confirmed cost owed* and *actual money paid* must be represented as different connected financial facts, so remaining supplier balances and both cost and cashflow reports remain accurate. Keep the user experience simple: a finance section with convenient provider ledger access, not duplicate manual spreadsheet entry.
- **Example:** An operator pays Baktun once for three reservation charges; the app stores **one real payment** and applies the appropriate amounts across the three rows. Partial allocations leave the unpaid balance; no second provider expense is created.
- **Approved clarification (same Decision 17):** The provider payment ledger must visibly distinguish **Reservation cost**, **Amount paid**, and **Remaining balance** per row. If a $100 reservation receives a $20 payment, the row remains **$100 cost / $20 paid / $80 remaining**; the cost is not rewritten to $80. Amount paid is derived from allocations; remaining balance is computed as effective cost minus allocations. Only a deliberate cost edit changes the total cost. Overpayments/credits and post-settlement cost adjustments are still open (`OQ-19`, `OQ-20`).
- **Do now:** Provide manual recording, multi-payable allocation, partial settlement and paid/outstanding totals. Persist actual supplier payments independently of operational reservation deletion (`DEC-015`).
- **Still open:** advance payments have since been approved in `DEC-018`; precise form/layout, overpayments *beyond* available credit, edits/reversals of settled transactions, bank reference/proof, refunds/credits, currency mismatches and due-date policy remain undecided (`OQ-19`, `OQ-20`, `OQ-23`).

### DEC-018 — Provider payment on account; allocation to future bookings — CONFIRMED

- **Approved:** Staff can record money **actually paid** to a provider today (cash or bank transfer) even when they do not yet wish to select reservation/payable items. Record one dated supplier payment and retain the **unallocated amount as available credit with that provider** (colloquially, “$500 in my Baktun account”). No fictional tour, provider cost, reservation or revenue is created just to hold this credit.
- **Approved:** At any later time, staff can allocate all or part of this existing provider credit toward one or more reservation/payable records, including bookings for tours that **have not yet taken place**. A planned payable linked to a finalized contract can be settled before the service date. Reuse the original outgoing payment, do **not** create a second payment/cash-out event.
- **Illustrative example:** Pay Baktun $500 now, with no allocation: **cash paid $500, unallocated provider credit $500**. When John's upcoming tour has a payable cost of $500, apply the credit: the tour's ledger shows **Reservation cost $500 / Amount paid $500 / Remaining balance $0**, and **unallocated credit $0**. Net provider account position is $0 in this example. The original cash-payment date is retained; applying credit later is not another expenditure.
- **Approved accounting distinction:** The provider account may simultaneously show **gross unpaid payables** and **unallocated credit**; the net account position can reflect both, while individual payable rows only count credit after an allocation. Credit is not supplier profit, revenue or a second supplier cost. `DEC-004` and `DEC-017` still apply to planned/confirmed/paid reporting.
- **Do now:** Permit partial or full *unallocated payment balances*, later allocations against existing/future-service payable entries, accurate paid/remaining columns, and provider-level credit vs balance visibility. Keep the provider account interface straightforward without forcing double-entry.
- **Still open:** Negative provider balances, overallocations, cross-currency credits, supplier refunds/credit notes, voids and edits to real payments, return of unapplied funds and rules for transferring credit between payees (`OQ-19`, `OQ-20`, `OQ-23`). Do not infer these extensions.

### DEC-019 — Release supplier allocation to credit vs actual refund — CONFIRMED

- **Approved:** When a reservation is cancelled/changed and an allocated provider payment must be made available for other bookings, staff can **release** that allocation back to the SAME provider's unapplied credit. This reallocates previously recorded money; it is **not** a new supplier payment, cash refund or reversal of the original paid transaction. It can later be allocated to another provider payable, including a future tour (`DEC-018`).
- **Approved separate action:** When the provider **actually returns money** to Tío Nenê (bank/cash), staff can record a **supplier refund received** separately from reallocating credit. It is cash received from the provider, **not customer revenue** and not a second supplier expense. Do not fabricate a refund on booking deletion or on an allocation release alone.
- **Illustrative case:** $500 paid to Baktun and allocated to John's tour. If Baktun agrees to retain the funds on account, release $500 to unapplied Baktun credit; the $500 original supplier payment remains and no cash is received. If Baktun actually returns $500, record the real refund and reduce the available provider credit/payment balance appropriately; don't falsely report that the money is still on deposit with Baktun.
- **Do now:** Implement logically distinct release-to-credit and record-refund paths, preserving the original supplier payment and a consistent allocation/credit balance. Keep routine controls minimal.
- **Not settled (`OQ-19`, `OQ-20`, `OQ-23`):** Supplier cancellation fees and whether the payable itself becomes zero, reduced or remains due; partial refund/fees, payment reversals, currency treatment, provider cross-credits and any automatic rules. Never silently forgive a payable when its payment allocation is released.


### DEC-020 — Set actual supplier cancellation charge by editing effective payable cost — CONFIRMED

- **Approved:** When a booked/reserved service is cancelled, the supplier may charge **nothing**, a **partial cancellation fee**, or the **full original reservation charge**. Authorized staff must be able to independently edit that specific supplier payable's **effective Reservation cost** to the amount the provider actually charges (for example, $100 original cost → $0, $30, or $100). The original contract, tour/service master, default supplier tariff and unrelated reservations must **not** change.
- **Approved separation of concerns:** A reservation's **booking status/cancellation**, the payable's **effective provider charge**, any existing **actual supplier payments/allocations**, available supplier credit, and any **real refund received** are separate facts/actions. Changing a payable cost does **not** invent a cash payment, cash refund, credit release, supplier-side cancellation or client refund. Existing real money transactions are preserved (`DEC-015`, `DEC-017`–`DEC-019`).
- **Example:** A cancelled Baktun tour previously had a $100 supplier cost. If Baktun confirms a $30 fee, authorized staff edit the **payable cost to $30**. Its remaining balance must accurately reflect existing allocations; if $0 was allocated, it is $30 due. If the original $100 was already allocated, the system must **not** silently erase or refund the $70 difference; how to handle the excess allocation will be decided next (`OQ-19`, `OQ-20`).
- **Do now:** Support per-payable effective cost override and correct financial reporting for $0/partial/full charges, respecting independent payable editing and separate settlement records. Keep the routine interface simple and show the adjusted payable as an expense, rather than automatically replacing upstream contract/reservation data.
- **Still open:** The precise prompted resolution of previously allocated payments **in excess of the revised cost**, partial refund allocation, cancellation fee notes/labels, and any automatic offer to release excess money to credit remain `OQ-19`/`OQ-20`. Do not automatically reallocate or refund money, or allow incorrect negative amounts to masquerade as outstanding debt. Client-side refunds and contract treatment remain separate (`OQ-27`).


### DEC-021 — Prompt to return excess allocated payments to provider credit — CONFIRMED

- **Approved:** When staff reduce a reservation's effective provider payable cost below the supplier payment value already allocated, identify the excess and offer to release that portion of the **existing payment allocation** back to the **same provider's unapplied credit**. Example: a Baktun reservation originally cost $100 and was paid/allocated $100; final supplier cancellation charge becomes $30; staff should be offered the option to release $70 to Baktun credit.
- **No automatic release:** Do not make the allocation change without operator action. The original $100 cash/bank payment remains recorded as paid to Baktun; releasing an allocation is **not** another payment or refund. A real supplier refund received remains a separate workflow (`DEC-019`). Never show a negative remaining balance as if it were debt.
- **Safe implementation:** Simple notice and action on the affected payable; do not add a cumbersome adjustment wizard or prominent historical audit UI. Preserve the agreed independent payment/booking edits.
- **Still open (`OQ-19`, `OQ-20`):** Unrelated financial reversals, complex allocation corrections, refunds and FX specifics. The example does not authorize the system to invent these rules.


### Confirmed post-v0.1.32 clarification — Cost unknown must not block finalized sales (not a new numbered DEC)

- **Confirmed:** A Seller can finalize a contract containing an unusual/custom tour or private yacht **even if the provider cost is still unknown**. Downstream arrival, reservation and linked planned-payable placeholder must be created; cost remains missing instead of a false zero.
- **Operational response:** Create an automatically derived **Cost Required** task until staff supplies the price; do not force a premature yacht quote or assume a master price exists.
- **Financial truth:** Reports may show known revenue and known costs but must clearly mark affected estimated margins and aggregates as **incomplete**, never as a fully calculated profit that implicitly assumes an unknown cost is $0.
- **Still open:** Actual tariffs per supplier/variant, yacht provider quote choices, age bands, future tariff configuration. These remain part of `OQ-10` and the separate source-data audit, but they do **not** block seller Finalize Sale (`CON-015`, `PAY-024`, `OPS-011`, `FIN-009`, `SC-090`–`092`).
