## 2. Supplier reservation form — standard fields, configurable provider variations

All supplier bookings, including those created after contract finalization, receive a **unique, auto-generated, stable internal reservation number**. The **supplier-issued confirmation number** is a **separate optional field**; it may arrive later. Standard fields: client name; hotel **effective on service date**; optional hotel room number; per-reservation adult, child and infant counts; selected service/tour; service date or date(s) where service actually spans dates; optional pickup time; optional meeting point; selected operator/provider; provider confirmation number; **supplier observations**; **internal private notes**. Reuse accurate existing contract/arrival data to prefill (never incorrectly global PAX across all services). Unknown pickup/meeting/confirmation information does not block creation.

**Privacy:** Supplier observations are eligible to appear on the specific reservation shared with that provider; **internal notes NEVER do**. Provider link reveals **only that one reservation** and the fields actually needed for it. Exclude CPF, customer sales prices/discounts, payment history, internal notes, supplier account ledger, unrelated bookings or contracts. High-entropy, separately scoped, revocable links and authentication/expiry decisions require developer threat review and implementation tests. Supplier access must not reveal private custom fields by default.

**Admin-only reservation management and no-code configuration:** Admin can add/relabel/order/hide/customize additional provider- or service-specific fields, data types, required state and visibility without code; new custom fields start private until deliberately disclosed. Do not allow Admin to delete core identity/audit/security/payment integrity invariants. Sellers manage customer contracts/receipts, **not supplier reservations**. Keep screen compact and history behind View History. WhatsApp messaging remains manual/optional; do not invent automated WhatsApp integration.

## 3. Xcaret future years — owner-managed seasons, no forced early choice

Keep **all 2027 dates selectable** until official 2027 closure/operating rules are configured, even when actual provider availability is unverified; never reuse 2026 closures, special Sundays or date windows as 2027 facts. For future-year dates, use **Xcaret supplier prices currently in the system**, but when the tour+zone can match both High and Regular existing rates and that future year is unconfigured, require **Admin to select/set a provisional active rate/season** (or record booking-specific choice). No automatic default of High vs Regular without Admin choice; the **2027 season decision is an in-app setup task, NOT a prerequisite to issuing this WIP developer spec**.

Admin can add/change future year season windows, weekdays, closures, exceptions and rates **whenever Xcaret supplies them**. December 15 annual update notice is an advisory reminder, not an edit gate. When costs are changed, offer explicit reviewed yes/no to apply new **supplier planned costs only** to **all future not-yet-commenced arrivals**, including ones already recorded/finalized. Preserve before/after, affected booking review status, and prior prices; never automatically modify customer-agreed contract selling values, client receipts, real supplier payments or historical signed PDFs. Preserve source 2026 Xcaret rule tables/images and exact 128 rate joins.

## 5. Unchanged critical boundaries

- Seller can independently finalize revised customer contract versions. Arrival visibly **Changed / Needs Attention** until Admin deliberately dismisses after reviewing affected records; no forced ledger equality or silent downstream cascade.
- Admin may edit bookings, contract operational items and supplier/payable costs including confirmed/paid items with **impact warnings, not reconciliation blocks**. Money movement corrections must preserve historical transactions and versions.
- Sellers/Admin can record unlimited separate customer receipts; **only Admin** reverses/corrects a receipt or records externally issued manual customer refunds. Refunds are separate dated money-out transactions.
- **Everything routine operational is Admin-configurable**, with clean everyday screens and detailed history hidden until requested. Do not confuse configurable data with arbitrary insecure executable scripting.
- Yacht transportation is **optional per booking**, not globally mandatory. Customer wallet visual design, legal rewrite (existing 18 Portuguese clauses are current), e-signature automation and auto-WhatsApp remain deferred.
