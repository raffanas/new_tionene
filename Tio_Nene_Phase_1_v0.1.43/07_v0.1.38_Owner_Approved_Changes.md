# 07 — Owner-approved changes since verified v0.1.37

**Captured:** 2026-10-20–21. **Authority:** owner-approved decisions in working discussion. This document supersedes only contradictory older wording. **No developer implementation has been inspected.**

## 1. Contract revisions — finalization separate from operations

- Sellers can create a new **version** of a finalized contract and **finalize the revised customer agreement themselves**. No prerequisite to fix an upstream/downstream mismatch or obtain Admin booking approval.
- Exactly **one arrival** remains linked to the contract across versions; preserve prior issued/signed PDF snapshots and linked customer receipts. Revised contract does not automatically alter already confirmed/paid/manually overridden bookings, payables, refunds or wallets.
- On revision finalization, mark affected arrival/contract **Changed / Needs Attention** until Admin explicitly dismisses it **after inspecting related arrival, reservation, payable and customer receipt records**, even if Admin deliberately leaves differing values. Do not auto-clear the attention flag when one child record happens to change.
- Admin can edit any operational record, **including paid/confirmed records**, at any time. Show a concise actionable **before-save affected-record warning**, but do not block a legitimate Admin edit merely due to divergence. Admin selects desired downstream changes; do not silently cascade, erase recorded payments, mutate an old signed PDF or pretend the change was reconciled.
- History and who/when/before/after values are retained in unobtrusive **View History**, not cluttering daily workflows. If proposed financial change would erase actual cash, enforce an auditable reversal/adjustment instead.

## 2. Precisely two roles; supplier links

- **Seller:** view/create/edit/draft/finalize/revise **any seller's** contracts, attach a returned signed file to the correct version, add and view customer receipt installments, see contract-level paid and outstanding balance; no supplier reservation creation/edit/confirmation, no supplier cost/payable/credit controls, no operational administration, no refund/correction privileges, no company financial reports by virtue of contract access. Seller receipt intake has **no deposit count, amount or time cutoff**.
- **Admin:** everything operational including reservations, supplier/payables/credits, manual receipt adjustments, refund logging, wallet preparation/edit, report access, admin settings, pricing, catalog and form configuration; ordinary app role and tenant access checks still apply.
- Supplier unique link exposes **only its one reservation** and the minimum relevant guest/service fields, including client name, hotel effective for service date, booked tour, service date, and adult/child/infant counts **when applicable**. No CPF, unrelated reservations, client-selling price, deposit/balance, company report or broad contract. Link needs high-entropy secret, revocation, backend scoping and an access audit. Exact supplier/service fields are owner **PENDING**, not inferred universally from examples (flight, passport, etc.). Provider links must not grant general Admin access.

## 3. Receipts, correction and rare refunds

- Both Seller and Admin may record **unlimited individual incoming receipt transactions**; each carries received date, currency and actual customer USD credit, amount, method, entering user, optional note, and a stable ID. Client contract total, net received and customer outstanding are calculated separately. BRL tender is still represented by the explicit credited USD value plus optional original tender note, **not double counted**.
- Only Admin may reverse/correct a recorded receipt. Original immutable transaction/audit context remains accessible in history; no deletion or secret rewrites to cash receipts.
- **Refunds are Admin-only and manual outside the app**: actual outgoing cash/bank/transfer first, then Admin logs a **separate dated negative money flow** linked to customer and contract, with amount, currency, date, method and reason. No in-app money transfer/automatic booking cancellation/customer selling-price modification/no multi-stage approval. Calculate net customer received = sums of credited receipts less completed refunds. If refund results in an apparent due balance, **flag for Admin review, do not assert debt automatically**. Preserve original receipt and visible finance summary simple.

## 4. No-hardcoded operational setup; app SUPER simple

- **P0:** Admin must self-serve changes to tour names/variants, descriptions, supplier prices, transport charges, season/calendar rules, hotel values, arrival and booking items, payments/corrections, contract content, wallets, and fields presented on relevant forms (including supplier-specific reservation forms). This includes **adding, renaming, ordering, optional/required and hiding configurable custom fields**, with field type/options/visibility where applicable, without routine developer intervention.
- Scope is **data-driven business forms and settings**, not replacing built-in authorization, money-integrity, immutable record identity or arbitrary executable business algorithms with unsafe user code. A configurable custom field must be stored and shown only in explicitly configured internal/customer/supplier views; default supplier link never exposes newly added sensitive fields accidentally. Settings changes prospective by default, not destructive migrations of past financial or signed documents.
- Routine screens: concise forms, a single current wallet, actionable labels, minimal prompts/clicks; auditing behind **View History**. Only real warnings prominently displayed.

## 5. Xcaret 2027 and future-year maintenance, plus pricing batch prompt

- **2026 unchanged.** Seller can choose **every calendar date in 2027** until specific 2027 rules are entered: do NOT inherit 2026 weekday closures, 2026 special Sundays or annual season date ranges. An unrestricted 2027 selector is *not proof* that Xcaret operates on a chosen date. Admin can enter/update weekly/special calendar constraints any time; preserve December 15 annual reminder as informational only.
- For future-year bookings, use **currently configured Xcaret supplier rates** as initial estimates instead of globally unknown prices. For a Tour ID/zone with multiple seasonal supplier rates but **no confirmed future season**, Admin must have an explicit, visible editable **fallback active Rate ID/default price** (or make a booking-specific documented selection); do not silently assume `High`, `Regular` or copy a 2026 date window. **Selecting the initial fallback policy is still pending owner confirmation**; the developer should support this configuration rather than hardcode a 2027 season.
- Admin may reprice source Xcaret supplier-cost rates at **any time**. After saving an updated catalog price, prompt **Apply new supplier costs to ALL not-yet-commenced future arrivals, including already entered/finalized contracts?** with a clear preview/count and explicit yes/no. When yes, update only eligible **supplier planned/payable cost values** while retaining prior snapshots and old source/selection history, and flag affected operational conflicts/paid allocations for Admin review. When no, keep existing booking costs. **NEVER automatically change existing customer contract selling values, receipts, prior signed PDFs, actual cash paid, prior-year source XLSX files or completed arrivals.** Admin may override individual bookings.
- A booking that has not commenced is evaluated against the arrival's actual commencement, not merely sale creation date. Developer must specify cutoff treatment and test boundary dates.

## 6. Ancillaries and wallets

- A private yacht booking **MAY OR MAY NOT** require separately arranged transport. It is **not** mandatory for every yacht; only an actual booking/service with Admin-configured **ancillary needed = yes** generates **Action Required** and the separate manual supplier reservation/payable, independent confirmation and cost. Admin can add other ancillary requirements later without code. Do not auto-book, auto-charge customer, or invent other mandatory extras.
- Existing wallet data/Ready/Mark Sent rules are retained. Its **visual PDF design is deferred to developer UI/PDF design/UAT**, not a source-logic or pre-handoff blocker; preserve editable wallet fields and one-current-wallet interface. Do not fabricate pickup or confirmation codes.

## 7. Client contract initial generation

- Use `Contract_Template/Tio_Nene_Contract_Source_Template_UNSIGNED.docx` as **source layout** and `Contract_Template/Contract_Generation_and_Field_Map.md` as binding contract. Four signed/real sample PDFs were compared privately to extract consistent fields/sections; DO NOT embed those customer PDFs in this ZIP.
- Keep owner-provided **18 Portuguese clauses unchanged** initially, but allow Admin to modify clauses/prospective template content through app later. Their legal correctness is NOT certified; legal revision explicitly deferred.
- Printed output dynamically repeats dated hotel stays, itinerary service/tour paragraphs (including with/without transfers), per-service adults/children/infants where available, optional includes/excludes, and a truthful aggregate customer payment summary. **Never expose internal per-tour negotiated line prices, provider costs, commissions, bank-internal notes or supplier-credit balances.**
- Any historic electronic signing hash/URL from the owner's original DOCX/signed PDF was **removed** from the reusable unsigned DOCX. Future genuinely signed versions may be uploaded and linked to the version; built-in signing/validation remains deferred.
